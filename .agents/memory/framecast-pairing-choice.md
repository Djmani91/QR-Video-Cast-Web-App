---
name: FrameCast short-code pairing choice
description: Owner's simplicity-first decision for pairing without QR or additional confirmation.
---

Use code-only pairing rather than QR scanning. Prefer two digits and expand to three when all two-digit codes are occupied. Keep existing sessions' codes unchanged.

**Why:** The owner explicitly chose short codes and no QR after being told about guessing risks, then requested three-digit overflow rather than refusing new pairings. They do not want extra customer passwords or confirmation steps.

**How to apply:** Keep phone, TV, discovery instructions, and API validation aligned. Do not describe the online pairing service as restricted to the customer's Wi-Fi: same-Wi-Fi usage alone does not provide that boundary. Retain invisible basic anti-spam checks; do not silently introduce extra approval screens.
