---
name: FrameCast ad placement
description: The agreed automatic advertising behavior for FrameCast mobile.
---

The user chose to keep only one automatic interstitial after each successful TV pairing. Do not add timed ads, extra banners, or a casting-time cutoff. Keep the TV video playing uninterrupted and keep media peer-to-peer and out of the ads SDK.

**Why:** The user rejected a five-minute casting limit because it would stop long videos, then explicitly chose to keep only the existing pairing ad.

**How to apply:** Trigger at most one interstitial after a successful pairing. If asked to add more placements later, agree on the placement first and review current AdMob policy: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6201362?hl=en
