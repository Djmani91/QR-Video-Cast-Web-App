---
name: FrameCast rewarded casting
description: The agreed optional ad and casting-time model for FrameCast.
---

Start a mobile casting session with one free hour. A rewarded ad must be started by the user, and only a completed earned reward adds one hour. A later completed ad may add another hour. Skipping or failing to load an ad does not remove time already granted. Never autoplay ads or send phone media to the ads SDK.

**Why:** The user chose an optional rewarded video and approved one free hour with one additional hour for each completed ad.

**How to apply:** Keep the countdown visible during mobile casting, run consent handling before ad requests, and preserve the direct phone-to-TV media path.
