# FrameCast for Android — shot script

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Your phone can pair with a TV, then send only the media you choose straight to the big screen.
- **Identity:** FrameCast's deep green and cream icon language with the selected purple, yellow, orange, blue, and red palette. Display face: Space Grotesk; supporting face: DM Sans; technical labels: JetBrains Mono.
- **Hero:** A bold Android phone frame and a wide TV receiver, with selected media visibly traveling between them.
- **Motion:** Punch-cut kinetic type; a drawn peer-to-peer connection path; interface cards that snap into a TV screen.
- **Arc:** Small-screen friction becomes an expansive shared picture; an explanatory midpoint makes privacy and selection legible; the final lockup turns the connection path into the FrameCast signal.
- **Payoff:** The phone and TV resolve as one direct line, followed by the actual FrameCast icon and the address 2tx1.com.
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical.
- **Runtime:** 27 seconds.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 0.00–4.286s / 4.286s — “YOUR MEDIA. BIG SCREEN.”
**Purpose:** Establish the simple transformation immediately: phone media becomes TV playback.
**Exact content and holds:** 0.00–0.45: “YOUR PHONE.” 0.45–1.30: “YOUR TV.” 1.30–3.75: “Your media, bigger.” Tiny footer “FRAMECAST FOR ANDROID” from 0.20–4.286. No narration.
**Composition:** Full-bleed deep evergreen field. A large phone silhouette begins cropped at lower left; a wide TV occupies the upper center. Cream typography at top, orange/yellow accents. Middle 80% safe zone.
**Artistic idea:** One still-photo tile is physically flung from the phone's glass and becomes the TV's picture, growing from a small rounded rectangle into a landscape window.
**Timed choreography:** 0.00 phone and “YOUR PHONE.” snap into view. 0.45s upper headline switches to “YOUR TV.” while TV feet unfold. 0.85s image tile ejects, trailing two colored signal arcs. 1.30s tile lands in the TV and expands to fill its screen. 2.15s a play triangle resolves inside the picture. 2.85s small caption arrives. 3.80s TV tilts toward lens; picture becomes a full-screen green frame, carrying the rounded rectangle to the pairing shot.
**Transition contract:** The TV-screen rounded rectangle stays centered and becomes the TV browser viewport in Shot 02; shape match plus subtle scale-through.
**Assets/technique:** CSS device geometry, SVG signal arcs, photo/video UI tile, no external imagery required. Supplied icon reserved for finale.
**Sound:** Instrumental only; cut at 4.286s, the eighth-beat mark.

### Shot 02 / 4.286–8.036s / 3.750s — “PAIR IN A MOMENT.”
**Purpose:** Explain pairing without implying automatic discovery: open the TV browser and use the same site.
**Exact content and holds:** “OPEN THE TV BROWSER” 4.48–6.00; `2tx1.com` 5.10–7.75; “Pair with the on-screen code.” 6.10–7.90.
**Composition:** A centered TV browser panel fills the frame vertically with a floating phone below; horizontal mint line connects their screens. Cream field with dark-green devices and purple/orange labels.
**Artistic idea:** The web address is a physical ribbon typing across the TV browser bar; five code digits become holes in a circular aperture that opens the media picker.
**Timed choreography:** 0.00 incoming rectangle sharpens as a TV browser window. 0.25 browser chrome draws in around it. 0.70 address types across the URL bar. 1.60 phone rises into lower third; five digit glyphs pop in separately at 1.85–2.45. 2.55 connecting line draws between devices. 3.05 digits spread into a ring; the ring rotates and opens like a lens. 3.48 lens aperture becomes the circular crop of the next shot.
**Transition contract:** The code ring morphs into the selected-media carousel aperture; transfer with circular shape match and split-panel wipe.
**Assets/technique:** Device/browser UI, kinetic type, SVG path draw, circular clip-path.
**Sound:** Instrumental; cut at 8.036s, the fifteenth eighth-beat mark.

### Shot 03 / 8.036–13.393s / 5.357s — “PICK WHAT PLAYS.”
**Purpose:** Highlight flexible inputs: photos, videos, and supported links.
**Exact content and holds:** “PHOTOS” 8.30–9.25; “VIDEOS” 9.25–10.25; “SUPPORTED LINKS” 10.25–11.75; “Send only what you select.” 11.55–13.10. UI labels “Photos · Videos · Links” visible in a queue stack from 10.60–13.10. At 12.45–13.10, small note: “Links open from their source on TV.”
**Composition:** Deep plum backdrop. Oversized phone at center, screen a media grid. Cards enter from three directions, each with distinct accent color (yellow photo, red video, blue link). Selected items stack in lower zone.
**Artistic idea:** The phone gallery behaves like a pinboard; three media cards are plucked from it, then snap into an ordered queue. The queue's connecting stroke becomes a beam.
**Timed choreography:** 0.00 circular code aperture opens on the phone gallery. 0.35 photo card punches forward and rotates 4°. 1.25 video card replaces its position on a hard beat. 2.25 blue link card swings in from upper right. 2.70 three cards fold into an ordered queue, each settling with a colored edge. 3.45 caption reveals below; one card advances to the front. 4.75 cards compress into a single orange-red transfer line; it exits upward/right on the last 0.4s.
**Transition contract:** The outgoing queue stroke lands at phone screen center and becomes the direct connection line in Shot 04; shape match plus a brisk background color wipe.
**Assets/technique:** Gallery cards and abstract media thumbnails built in CSS/SVG, no claim that every link/site works.
**Sound:** Instrumental; cut at 13.393s, the twenty-fifth eighth-beat mark.

### Shot 04 / 13.393–18.214s / 4.821s — “DIRECT. DEVICE TO DEVICE.”
**Purpose:** Make the peer-to-peer relationship and privacy boundary clear.
**Exact content and holds:** “PHONE” 13.65–17.90 under left device; “TV” 13.65–17.90 under right; “Selected media travels directly.” 14.45–17.90; small note “No media upload to FrameCast.” 15.75–17.90.
**Composition:** Cream background. Phone large at lower left, TV large at upper right. Thick segmented line runs diagonally between device screens with media packets moving along it. No cloud icon, server rack, or relay node. Purple, blue, red, yellow packets.
**Artistic idea:** The selected media is shown as a real stream of small, differently shaped pieces traveling screen-to-screen along one unbroken path. Pairing signal is represented separately as a tiny dashed control pulse.
**Timed choreography:** 0.00 phone/TV appear at opposing edges as the outgoing line arrives. 0.35 labels snap under each device. 0.70 first packet crosses; second and third follow at 0.25s spacing. 1.65 caption enters on a mask from the path's midpoint. 2.55 a dashed pairing pulse travels back in the opposite direction, visually distinct from the solid media flow. 3.20 privacy footnote appears. 3.95 camera pulls back to show the uninterrupted route; packets continue moving during the handoff. Last beat sends one packet to fill the TV.
**Transition contract:** TV-bound packet expands into the TV playback surface; scale-through plus a vertical split and color inversion.
**Assets/technique:** Animated SVG paths/dashes, flat packet shapes, device UI; explicitly no cloud visual.
**Sound:** Instrumental; cut at 18.214s, the thirty-fourth eighth-beat mark.

### Shot 05 / 18.214–22.500s / 4.286s — “MADE FOR THE BIG SCREEN.”
**Purpose:** Show the payoff and useful playback behaviors: video, ordered playlist, photo browsing.
**Exact content and holds:** “PLAY ON THE TV.” 18.45–19.95; “Next up, automatically.” 20.15–21.15; “Browse a photo batch in-session.” 21.40–22.40.
**Composition:** TV dominates center, picture window about 78% frame width. Deep blue background with yellow/red geometric media markers. Phone appears as a narrow control presence on the lower left, then becomes a filmstrip.
**Artistic idea:** One video frame plays, becomes a second queued frame in a continuous playlist handoff, then the TV screen folds into a three-photo browse strip; the TV never shrinks away.
**Timed choreography:** 0.00 packet fills the TV screen; image motion implied by animated landscape layers. 0.45 headline cuts in above. 1.35 video scrub line advances and a second clip's thumbnail slides into a small upcoming slot. 2.10 first frame cuts to next clip inside the same TV glass. 2.65 sentence changes to “Next up, automatically.” 3.15 screen splits into three photo panes; captions change to browsing copy. 3.95 photo strip folds into one rounded frame that becomes the app-icon screen.
**Transition contract:** The rounded TV frame collapses into the supplied app icon's inner screen geometry; fold-in plus radial aperture close.
**Assets/technique:** CSS TV, animated playback still abstraction, filmstrip/photo-grid, supplied icon at the ending.
**Sound:** Instrumental; cut at 22.500s, the forty-second beat.

### Shot 06 / 22.500–27.000s / 4.500s — “FRAMECAST.”
**Purpose:** Resolve with privacy, identity, and the website.
**Exact content and holds:** “FRAMECAST” 23.00–26.90; “Your media stays off FrameCast servers.” 23.80–26.90; `2tx1.com` 24.90–26.90. A small qualifier “Direct connection requires a compatible network path.” 24.10–26.90.
**Composition:** Warm cream end field. Supplied app icon centered, slightly above center, 42% frame width. A colored path loops around the icon once then tucks into its broadcast mark. Dark green typography and all five palette accents.
**Artistic idea:** The line that carried media in Shot 04 retracts and draws the orange broadcast motif adjacent to the original, untouched icon.
**Timed choreography:** 0.00 icon's screen crops open to reveal its exact original art. 0.45 connection line curls once around it, then retracts. 0.60 wordmark forms beneath from tight tracking. 1.25 privacy sentence appears. 1.70 qualifier arrives small but readable. 2.40 web address lands in a yellow tab. 3.75s final lockup scales to a stable hold until 4.50s, then the background circle grows to full evergreen, matching the opening's first frame.
**Transition contract:** Final evergreen field and TV-screen rounded rectangle align with the opening's phone-to-TV hook; a circle-to-rounded-screen morph closes the loop.
**Assets/technique:** Original `attached_assets/framecast-app-icon.png` via module import; do not alter its logo colors. Type lockup is plain product name, not a fabricated wordmark logo.
**Sound:** Instrumental; resolve at 27.00s.
