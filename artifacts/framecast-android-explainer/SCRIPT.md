# FrameCast for Android — product walkthrough

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** Pair once, then choose photos, videos, or a Premium phone-screen mirror from FrameCast and play them on the TV.
- **Identity:** Preserve the existing FrameCast evergreen / warm-cream foundation and the selected purple, yellow, orange, blue, and red accents. Space Grotesk display, DM Sans support, JetBrains Mono technical labels. Keep the original supplied icon unchanged.
- **Hero:** Actual FrameCast Android screens, staged inside one consistent handset. The only TV visual is the browser in the one-time address-entry scene.
- **Motion:** App-screen match cuts, restrained interface callouts, a pairing-code handoff, and a soft signal stroke.
- **Arc:** Pair at the TV once → return to the app → choose / control media → unlock the Premium-only mirror → keep casting in the background → disconnect.
- **Payoff:** The disconnect action resolves to the original FrameCast app icon and the familiar site address.
- **Format:** Preserve 9:16.
- **Runtime:** 62 seconds, eight scenes. Auto-playing and looped; all interface taps are illustrative artwork, not interactive controls.

## Product truth / depiction rules
- The TV browser appears only in Scene 02, where the address is typed once. Do not imply FrameCast launches a Samsung browser. Return to the Android app and pair using the TV QR code or five-digit code.
- No TV hardware, receiver, or TV-browser visual appears in the opening or after Scene 02. All later walkthrough scenes stay on FrameCast phone-app screens.
- Photos and videos are separate media tabs. One of each type is free per TV session; a rewarded ad unlocks one additional item of that same type. Premium allows up to 20 photos and 20 videos per TV session.
- Photo controls: browse the photo batch from the phone, zoom in/out, reset to Fit, and pan. Video controls: play/pause, −10s/+10s, timeline seek, volume down/up, mute, stop, and playlist navigation where available.
- Android screen mirroring is Premium-only. Do not show a free minute or rewarded mirror unlock. Show Android screen-capture permission before mirroring.
- Phone content and notifications may be visible on the TV. Supported app audio may be sent. Protected apps may block video/audio. FrameCast does not record microphone audio.
- Premium background casting starts automatically when connected to a TV and continues when FrameCast is backgrounded, with an Android casting notification. Free casting stops when FrameCast is backgrounded.
- Keep every phone interface faithful to the source app's labels and palette. No invented settings or unsupported promises.
